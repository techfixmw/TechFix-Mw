About TechFix Mw

TechFix Mw is a technology and technical services business based in Chigumula, Blantyre, Malawi. We provide web design and development services, vehicle tracking system installation, electrical appliance repairs, and computer hardware and software support.

Our goal is to help individuals and businesses solve technical challenges through dependable service, practical solutions, and professional digital experiences.

We combine hands-on technical skills with clear communication so customers understand their options and receive solutions that fit their needs and budget.

Mission

To provide accessible, reliable, and professional technology, web development, installation, and repair services that help individuals and businesses work more efficiently.

Vision

To become a trusted technology and repair services provider in Malawi, recognized for quality workmanship, innovation, and customer satisfaction.

Our Values

The principles that guide every project and repair we undertake.

  • Professionalism
  • Reliability
  • Innovation
  • Quality workmanship
  • Customer satisfaction
  • Continuous learning
